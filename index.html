FIELD MANUAL

Strength in numbers.

  1. Command the lane. Drag to the lane you want, tap arrows, or use ← → / A D. Your squad shoots the closest living hostile in its lane and weapon range. The warlord can be targeted from either lane.
  2. Dodge return fire. A red lane marker warns of an incoming burst. Switch lanes before it lands, or kill the shooter to cancel it. Enemy fire costs 1–6 squad strength; a barrier absorbs one burst. Dodging keeps your barrier.
  3. Recruit and rearm. + adds strength, × multiplies it, − removes it; strength caps at 300. A MECH adds 20 strength and +3 damage to every volley (up to three); on losses, infantry strength goes first. Pick up a short-range heavy scattergun or long-range plasma rifle; each replaces your current gun.
  4. Kill before contact. Shots reduce real HP. A dead enemy costs no strength or shield; surviving enemies cost only their remaining armor. Saws take 35%. A barrier blocks one hit, but not warlord contact. A ranged-killed warlord has no contact hit.
  5. Recover salvage. A defeated hostile awards +2 salvage; the warlord awards +5, once per kill. Ranged kills pay immediately. Survive the full 20–24-encounter route to finish the sector.

Watch the shoulders. Your squad fires at roadside zombies as well as its main target. Steer toward a horde to focus fire. Each zombie has 1 HP; survivors take up to 3 crew as you pass (less across the road). A barrier blocks one ambush. Clearing hordes earns kill score, not salvage.

Rearm halfway. Each sector takes about 2½ minutes of active play. At the safe haven, your squad stops and your score is banked. Buy permanent damage or aim ranks, or resupply infantry, mechs, a tank, and a shield for this run only. Continue Run with the same crew. Shopping time does not advance the run; purchased recruits do not earn points when the run ends.

P, Space, or Escape pauses a run. Switching apps pauses automatically.
